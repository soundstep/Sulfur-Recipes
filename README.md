# Sulfur Recipes

Sulfur Recipes is a small companion app for the game [SULFUR](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2124120/SULFUR/). It lets you browse and manage in‑game recipes in a simple, focused interface.

## What it does

- Keeps SULFUR recipes in one place  
- Makes it easy to look up what you need for specific items  
- Lets you quickly scan ingredients and results  
- Gives you a clearer overview of what you can craft next  

## Using the app

Open the web app in your browser:

- Website: [sulfur-recipes.pages.dev](https://sulfur-recipes.pages.dev)
- Data from the [SULFUR Wiki](https://sulfur.wiki.gg/)

Browse or search for a recipe, check the requirements, and continue your run.
